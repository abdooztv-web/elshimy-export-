Getting Started

How do I get a quote from El-Shimy?expand_more

Simply fill in our online quote form with your product, destination country, and quantity. We respond within 24 business hours with a competitive price. Alternatively, message us directly on WhatsApp for the fastest response.

What is the minimum order quantity?expand_more

Minimum order quantities depend on the product and destination. For most fresh produce we work with full pallet loads as a minimum for sea freight. For air freight, smaller quantities are available for premium markets. Contact us and we'll advise the most economical option for your volume.

Which countries does El-Shimy export to?expand_more

We export to 85+ countries across:

  • Europe — Germany, UK, Netherlands, France, Italy, Spain, and more
  • Gulf — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain
  • Russia and the CIS countries
  • Asia — Singapore, Malaysia, China, Japan
  • Africa — and beyond
Does El-Shimy also import produce into Egypt?expand_more

Yes. Alongside our export operations, we manage strategic import of high-demand exotic fruits and essential produce into the Egyptian market. We handle sourcing, quality verification, customs clearance, and domestic distribution. Contact us to discuss your import needs.

Products & Quality

How many products does El-Shimy export?expand_more

We export 16 certified fresh produce varieties across four seasons — Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter. This means we can offer continuous supply partnerships with year-round product variety. See our full Products page or download our catalogue.

What quality standards do your products meet?expand_more

All El-Shimy produce is sourced from GlobalGAP-certified farms and complies with:

  • EU Food Safety Regulations
  • HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points)
  • International Phytosanitary Standards (IPPC)
  • Destination-specific import requirements

We provide complete documentation for every shipment, with zero border rejections as our standard.

Can I request a product sample before placing an order?expand_more

Yes. We can arrange sample shipments for qualified buyers. The cost of sampling is typically covered in the first commercial order. Contact our team via WhatsApp or email to arrange this — sample availability depends on the current season.

What packaging options are available?expand_more

Packaging varies by product but we offer standard industry cartons as well as custom branded packaging for established partners. Typical options include punnets (250g, 500g, 1kg), cartons (5kg, 10kg, 15kg, 18kg), and bulk sacks for root vegetables. Specific options are listed on each product card on our Products page.

Logistics & Shipping

What shipping methods does El-Shimy use?expand_more

We use three main freight methods depending on the product and destination:

  • Sea freight — Refrigerated (reefer) containers for most bulk orders. Most cost-effective for large volumes.
  • Air freight — For premium perishables or time-sensitive orders. Higher cost but preserves maximum freshness.
  • Road freight — For regional and overland destinations.
How is the cold chain maintained during shipping?expand_more

Cold chain integrity is a top priority. From harvest, produce is cooled to appropriate storage temperatures within hours. We use climate-controlled packing facilities, pre-cooled reefer containers, and partner with certified logistics providers. Temperature logs are available for every sea freight shipment.

What are typical lead times from order to delivery?expand_more

Lead times depend on destination and freight method:

  • Gulf countries — 7–14 days (sea), 2–4 days (air)
  • Europe — 14–21 days (sea), 3–5 days (air)
  • Russia — 18–25 days (sea)
  • Asia — 20–30 days (sea)

We provide tracking and regular updates throughout transit.

Who handles customs clearance at the destination?expand_more

El-Shimy prepares all export documentation on the Egyptian side — phytosanitary certificates, health certificates, packing lists, bill of lading, and certificate of origin. For import customs at the destination, we work with your local customs broker or can recommend trusted partners in major markets. CIF and FOB terms are both available.

Certifications & Compliance

Is El-Shimy GlobalGAP certified?expand_more

Yes. We work exclusively with GlobalGAP-certified farms. This certification ensures that our agricultural practices meet internationally recognised standards for food safety, environmental sustainability, and worker welfare. Certification documentation is available on request for every product.

Are your products approved for the European Union market?expand_more

Yes. We comply fully with EU food safety regulations, including maximum residue limits (MRL), phytosanitary requirements, and labelling standards. Our track record in the EU market includes zero border rejections. We are experienced in working with major European supermarket chains and distributors.

Can you supply organic certified produce?expand_more

We have access to organic certified farms for select product lines. Availability depends on the season and product. Please specify your requirement when requesting a quote and we will confirm current availability and certification documentation.

Pricing & Payment

How is pricing determined?expand_more

Pricing is based on current market conditions, season, product variety, quantity, packaging specifications, and freight method. We provide transparent, competitive quotes. Volume buyers and long-term partners benefit from preferential pricing and priority allocation during peak season.

What payment terms does El-Shimy accept?expand_more

We work with standard international trade payment terms including:

  • Letter of Credit (LC) — preferred for new partners
  • Telegraphic Transfer (TT) — wire transfer
  • Documentary Collection (CAD)

Payment terms are discussed and agreed during the quotation process. For established partners, open account terms may be available.

Does El-Shimy offer long-term supply contracts?expand_more

Yes — we actively seek long-term supply partnerships. Annual or multi-season contracts offer volume price guarantees, priority farm allocation, and dedicated account management. We believe the best partnerships grow stronger over time. Contact us to discuss a supply agreement tailored to your business.
