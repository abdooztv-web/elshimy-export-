Sourcing & Export

Sourcing & Export Services

One point of contact for buying Egyptian produce. We source from partner farms and packhouses and coordinate quality, documents and shipping to your specification.

Request a QuoteWhat we coordinate

A partner-based model

EL-SHIMY was established in 2016. We do not own farms or packing facilities: we work with partner farms and packhouses and manage the buying process on your behalf, so you deal with a single trading partner.

What we coordinate

  • Product & variety sourcing

    Fresh fruits and vegetables, plus selected IQF-frozen strawberries and berries.

  • Specification

    Variety, size, grade, packing, labelling and private-label needs.

  • Quality coordination

    Inspection at packing and pre-shipment checks agreed with you.

  • Export documentation

    Commercial and shipping documents, phytosanitary and origin certificates as required.

  • Shipping

    Sea, air and land freight with customs arrangements.

Who we work with

  • Importers
  • Wholesalers
  • Hotels & hospitality
  • Food distributors
  • Food manufacturers

Gulf countries are our first priority, followed by Asia.

How a quote works

Prices are quoted per request because they depend on season, volume, packing and freight.

  1. 1

    Send your enquiry

    Product, variety, size, packing, quantity, destination port and Incoterm.

  2. 2

    We confirm availability

    Matching partner farms and packhouses to your specification.

  3. 3

    Receive a written offer

    Specification, packing, terms and price in one document.

  4. 4

    Plan the shipment

    Packing, freight and documents coordinated to your schedule.

Tell us what you are looking for

Send the product, quantity and destination and we will come back with a specification-based offer.
