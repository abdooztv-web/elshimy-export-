Choose the mode that fits the product

Each mode suits different products, volumes and timings. We help you choose, then plan packing and documents around it.

Sea Freight

Temperature-controlled (reefer) containers for suitable fruit, vegetable and frozen programmes, shipped from Egyptian ports where available.

  • Reefer containers for chilled or frozen cargo, as suits the product
  • Pallet loading planned with your packing specification
  • Set-points and ventilation agreed per commodity
Ask about sea freight

Air Freight

Air transport for suitable time-sensitive, perishable shipments such as fresh strawberries, mangoes or stone fruit.

  • Suited to highly perishable or urgent lines
  • Packing and pre-cooling planned for air handling
  • Routing and cut-offs depend on airline and airport
Ask about air freight

Land Freight

Regional overland transport where road routes are practical for the destination.

  • Refrigerated or ventilated trucks, as the product requires
  • Useful for neighbouring and regional destinations
  • Border and permit requirements confirmed per route
Ask about land freight

Door-to-door delivery can be arranged where available. Transit times, costs and availability are quoted per enquiry; we do not publish fixed figures.

Customs clearance & documents

We coordinate export clearance in Egypt and the paperwork your importer needs. The exact set depends on the product and the destination, and not every document applies to every shipment.

We confirm the document list with you before loading. Please also check requirements with your customs authority, as rules differ by country and can change.

  • Commonly requested

    Commercial invoice

    Describes the goods and the agreed value for customs.

  • Commonly requested

    Packing list

    Itemises cartons, weights and pallets in the shipment.

  • If required

    Certificate of origin

    Confirms Egyptian origin where the contract or destination requires it.

  • If applicable

    Phytosanitary certificate

    Plant-health document for fresh produce, where the importing country requires it.

  • Per shipment

    Bill of lading / air waybill

    The transport document issued by the carrier for the shipment.

  • Destination-specific

    Destination-specific documents

    Extra certificates, permits or labels your importing authority asks for.
