Details buyers ask for

Varieties

Seedless, easy-peel mandarin types. Ask about the specific variety, colour and seed expectations for your market.

Sizes & grading

Sized by count per carton in bands agreed per order (for example 72–125).

Packaging

Cartons or crates, palletised; mesh bags for dry vegetables. Exact format and weight agreed per order.

Storage & shipping

Shipped in temperature-controlled (reefer) containers or by air freight, depending on the commodity, transit time and destination. Set-points and ventilation are agreed per product.

Documentation

Commonly requested: commercial invoice, packing list, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and any importer-specific documents. We confirm the exact set with you before shipment.
