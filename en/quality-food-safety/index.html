Our quality process

Five steps from the buyer's requirements to a documented shipment.

  1. 01

    Define Specifications

    Agree variety, size, grade, packaging, labelling and buyer requirements in writing.

  2. 02

    Select Suppliers

    Coordinate with suitable partner farms and packhouses for your product and season.

  3. 03

    Inspect and Verify

    Coordinate the checks and records agreed for your order, at packing and before loading.

  4. 04

    Plan Handling and Transport

    Coordinate pre-cooling and cold-chain requirements when they apply to the product and route.

  5. 05

    Prepare Documentation

    Confirm the required documents and destination-specific requirements before shipment.

Specifications, records and shipment documents, in one place

Buyer confidence comes from clear information. For each order we can organise the following, as agreed:

  • Written product specification

    Variety, size, grade, packing and labelling.

  • Supplier information

    Which partner farm and packhouse supplies the order.

  • Inspection records

    Check results or photos, where agreed for the order.

  • Shipment documents

    Invoice, packing list and certificates as required.

What you receive depends on the order and what we agree with you in advance.

Third-party inspection & additional standards

If your programme needs an independent inspection company, retailer requirements or other standards, tell us at enquiry stage so it can be built into the plan and costed. We discuss additional standards only where they can be evidenced.

What this means in practice

  • We coordinate with partner farms and packhouses; we do not operate our own farms or packhouses.
  • We do not operate our own laboratory.
  • We do not guarantee zero defects. Tolerances are agreed in the specification for each order.
