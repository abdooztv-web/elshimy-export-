Our Legacy

From Giza to the Globe

El-Shimy for Trading & Agencies was founded in 2016 with a clear purpose: to represent the world-class quality of Egyptian agricultural produce on the global stage.

Based in Beverly Hills, Giza, we collaborate with Egypt's top investors and farming partners to supply international markets with the freshest, most reliable produce available. Every shipment carries the pride of Egyptian agriculture and the professionalism of an experienced global trade partner.

We have grown from a regional distributor to a worldwide export operation — moving over two billion items to 85+ countries across Europe, the Gulf, Russia, Africa, and Asia.

"Commitment to quality is not just a promise — it is our heritage and the foundation of every relationship we build."
